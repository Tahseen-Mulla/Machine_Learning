What is Association Rule Mining?

Association rule mining finds items that tend to co-occur in the same transaction — the classic "customers who bought X also bought Y" pattern from market-basket analysis. Every rule {A} → {B} is scored on three measures:

Support = P(A and B) — how often the combination occurs.
Confidence = P(B | A) — how reliable the rule is.
Lift = P(B | A) / P(B) — how much more likely B is, given A, than by chance. Lift > 1 means a genuine positive association.

The Apriori algorithm speeds this up by first pruning itemsets that don't meet the minimum support threshold, since no rule built from an infrequent itemset can be useful. Here, with only a handful of items, we simply enumerate every subset directly.
