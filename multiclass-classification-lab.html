What is Multi-class Classification?

Many real problems involve more than two categories — for example classifying an email as personal, work, or spam. Multi-class classifiers extend binary methods either by training one classifier per class (one-vs-rest) or by directly generalising to K classes (as softmax regression does).

Nearest-centroid rule (used here):
1. Compute the mean position (centroid) of each class's points.
2. For any new point, predict the class whose centroid is closest.

This simple rule already illustrates the core idea: the decision regions are the areas of the plane closer to one centroid than any other — a Voronoi partition of the feature space.
