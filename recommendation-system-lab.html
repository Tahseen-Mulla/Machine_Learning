What is a Recommendation System?

User-based collaborative filtering recommends items by finding users with similar taste and suggesting what they liked. Similarity between two users' rating vectors is commonly measured with cosine similarity:

cos(u, v) = (u · v) / (||u|| · ||v||)

A value near 1 means very similar rating patterns; near 0 means unrelated tastes.

To recommend items to a target user, the system looks at items the user hasn't rated, and scores each one as a similarity-weighted average of how other users rated it — giving more weight to more-similar users. The main weakness of this approach is cold start: a brand-new user or item with no ratings yet has nothing to compare against.
